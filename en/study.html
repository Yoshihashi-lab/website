Study

Research Themes

Approach 01
~ Accelerator Physics ~
(Development of Neutron Generators)
From fundamental physics research to medical applications, we develop a variety of neutron generation technologies tailored to specific purposes. Our laboratory is equipped with NUANS, an accelerator designed for BNCT, which we apply to fundamental BNCT research. We also utilize other facilities (e.g., MS10 electron accelerator).
Approach 02
~ Neutronics ~
(Advanced Neutron Measurement Technologies)
The ability to accurately measure neutrons is the foundation of all applications. Our laboratory develops advanced neutron measurement technologies, connecting seemingly different fields—from dose evaluation in BNCT to environmental assessment in fusion reactors—through the common language of "measurement".
Approach 03
~ Cell damage Analysis ~
(Biological Effects & Applications)
We evaluate how neutrons, used for various purposes, actually affect biological organisms. This "observing" approach not only ensures radiation safety but also bridges the gap to life sciences and further safety evaluations.
