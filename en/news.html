Conference Prof. Sachiko Yoshihashi gave a presentation on "Introduction of NUANS and BNCT for companion animals" at the BNCT International Meeting at KUMATRI.

Prof. Sachiko Yoshihashi gave a presentation at the "BNCT International Meeting at KUMATRI". Her talk, titled "Introduction of NUANS and BNCT for companion animals", introduced the status of the Nagoya University Accelerator-driven Neutron Source (NUANS) project and the research on applying Boron Neutron Capture Therapy (BNCT) as a cancer treatment for pets (companion animals).
